Investigations

When a dispute turns on what can be proved.

We are instructed before proceedings, during them, and after judgement. The work fills the gaps a legal team cannot close from the file alone — where assets went, who really controls a counterparty, whether a document is what it purports to be.

Findings are graded and sourced so they can be tested. We would rather hand you a narrow conclusion that holds than a broad one that does not.

Asset tracing
Value across jurisdictions and the structures used to hold it — corporate layers, nominees, real property, beneficial interests.
Enforcement
Where assets sit now, who controls them, and what has moved since proceedings began.
Fraud
Misappropriation, procurement manipulation, and the related-party arrangements that carry the proceeds.
Financial crime
Money-laundering typologies, layering through opaque jurisdictions, and the professionals who enable it.
Internal investigations
Conduct enquiries run at arm's length from the business, where independence is the point.
Arbitration support
Background on parties, funders and experts; evidence gathering in seats where access is difficult.
Sanctions exposure
Whether ownership or control brings a counterparty within a designation, and how the chain is constructed.
Evidence authentication
Where a document or image is disputed, what its own provenance supports and what independent sources corroborate.
How we work
Instructed by advisors
We take instructions from the legal team and work on the footing they set, so that privilege is preserved where it can be.
Conflict-free
Cleared before we accept. We do not act on both sides of a matter, and we will tell you plainly when we must decline.
Graded, not asserted
Every source carries a grade and every enquiry a record. Where a finding rests on one uncorroborated account, the report says so on its face.
Lawfully obtained
No pretexting into regulated data, no unlawful interception. Material obtained improperly is worth nothing to you, and we will not gather it.
Alongside your team
We work with overseas counsel, communications advisers and security teams where a matter requires it, and stay in our lane.
